# Crit 5 reflection

**What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?**

Realising that "the round-clear feature doesn't exist" and "the round-clear
feature exists but can never trigger" look identical from the outside — a
screenshot of a nearly-full ring and a forced loss doesn't tell you which one
you're looking at. I'd asked for a win condition to be designed, assuming
there wasn't one. The breakthrough wasn't spotting a bug so much as refusing
the first framing of my own complaint: asking why an already-built thing
wasn't working, instead of asking for a new one to be built on top of it,
turned what I thought was a missing feature into a small, honest fix to an
existing check that didn't match how people actually click.

**What did this work change about who I want to be as a developer?**

I noticed I kept handing over symptoms — "it feels unfair," "round 3 is
impossible" — rather than diagnoses, and that turned out to be the more
useful thing to give. I couldn't have named the actual cause, an even-packing
assumption that real, unevenly-timed play never satisfies, but describing
what playing it actually felt like, precisely and without guessing at the
fix myself, was enough for the real cause to get found each time. I want to
keep trusting that a clear account of what happened when I use the thing
is worth more than a guess at how to fix it.
